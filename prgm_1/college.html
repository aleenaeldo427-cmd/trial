ABOUT THE COLLEGE

Mar Baselios Institute of Technology and Science (MBITS) is a self-financing engineering institution established in 2009 and located in Nellimattom, Kothamangalam, Kerala. Run by the Mar Thoma Cheriyapally under the Mar Basil Group of Institutions, the college is approved by the AICTE, affiliated with the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), and holds prestigious NBA and NAAC accreditations. The campus offers a comprehensive range of academic programs, including B.Tech, M.Tech, BCA, MCA, MBA, and Ph.D. research courses, alongside a dedicated polytechnic diploma wing. Known for its strong focus on practical learning, MBITS features modern smart classrooms, specialized laboratories, and an active placement cell that drives campus recruitment with leading technical companies. Set in a peaceful campus environment alongside National Highway 49, it blends rigorous engineering education with vibrant student clubs, technical festivals, and cultural events.MBITS college is NBA accredited. Reviews point to a supportive faculty.
